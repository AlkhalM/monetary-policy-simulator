Connections to class weeks and crises

Key distinction for the midterm

Tools are specific actions or facilities. Instruments are the immediate rates/reserve quantities influenced. Intermediate targets include financial conditions, credit, monetary aggregates and expectations. Goals are final economic outcomes.

This is a conceptual teaching simulator. Real outcomes depend on the amount, timing, credibility, banking conditions, exchange-rate regime, supply shocks and transmission delays.
